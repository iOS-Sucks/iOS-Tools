games you can host yourself

Most "unblocked games" mirrors are just open-source HTML5 games on static hosting — host your own copy and skip the ads and trackers.

  1. Fork one of the repos below (free GitHub account).
  2. Repo → Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → default branch, / (root).
  3. Open https://<you>.github.io/<repo> — that copy is yours.
  • 2048 — sliding puzzle (MIT) ↗
  • t-rex runner — offline dino game (BSD-3) ↗
  • hextris — fast hexagon puzzler (check repo license) ↗

Respect each repo's license. A school network can still block github.io — that's the network owner's call, not something to work around.

school-day setup

Prep your own devices the night before — your hardware, your network, no school systems involved.

Go offline-ready.

  1. Media: download playlists, podcasts, and videos over home Wi-Fi (Spotify / Apple Music / Netflix all support offline downloads).
  2. Reading: save pages to Safari Reading List (downloads automatically) or keep this site via Share → Add to Home Screen — it runs without a network once loaded.
  3. Games: grab App Store games that support offline play before you leave.

Hotspot basics (your data, your devices).

  1. iPhone: Settings → Personal Hotspot → Allow Others to Join, with a strong password.
  2. Join your iPad or laptop to it instead of public Wi-Fi. Video eats gigabytes — enable Low Data Mode on the connected device.
  3. Hotspotting drains batteries fast — pack a cable or power bank.

Battery + focus.

  1. Top up overnight; switch on Low Power Mode (Settings → Battery) when heading out.
  2. Make a School Focus (Settings → Focus): silence games and social apps during class, still allow calls from family.
  3. Free space for downloads: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → offload unused apps.

Everything here runs on hardware and networks you own. Nothing touches school management or filters.

school-managed device (Jamf & co.)

What you can actually do with a supervised iPad you don't administer.

See what's installed. Settings → General → VPN & Device Management lists every profile. Supervised devices say so at the top of Settings. Looking is read-only — always safe.

Buying used? Check before paying. Setup must never show a "Remote Management" enrollment screen — if it does, the device still belongs to an org and only their admin can release it. Have the seller remove it from their Apple ID / console in front of you, or walk away.

Yours to manage? If you hold the admin credentials, remove profiles in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management or unenroll from the management console. Without credentials there is no legitimate removal path.

School profile on your own device? If it's marked removable, delete it yourself: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap the profile → Remove Management. If it demands credentials you don't have, only the admin can remove it — dodging it while still enrolled is still a policy violation.

Day to day: keep a personal device for anything school policy blocks, and use offline-capable tools — this whole site runs without a network once loaded (Share → Add to Home Screen, or Ctrl/Cmd+S on desktop).
